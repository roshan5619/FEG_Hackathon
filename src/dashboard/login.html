O ovim računima / About these accounts

The usernames and display names are invented labels. They are attached to pre-hashed anonymous player identifiers from the FEG sample. The export contains no name, age, gender, location or any other personal attribute — so none can be revealed here. The play history behind each account is real; the person is not.

Passwords are stored as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes with per-user salts — no plaintext credential exists in the repository. The session cookie is HMAC-signed, httponly and expiring. This is prototype auth: no rate limiting, lockout, rotation or MFA, and docs/compliance-note.md says so.

Two accounts demonstrate the responsible-play gates, and they behave differently on purpose. davor.z has unverified age and is refused sign-in — Croatia's Act on Measures for Socially Responsible Organisation of Games of Chance requires the check before play is allowed. lucija.h is self-excluded and can sign in — self-exclusion blocks inducements, not account access — but receives zero recommendations and the required support surfaces instead.
