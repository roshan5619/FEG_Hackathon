Popularno is the row psk.hr ships today (Najigranije). We kept it: on next-game prediction, global popularity beat collaborative filtering outright — 0.305 vs 0.056 NDCG@10. Replacing it would have made the lobby worse.

Nastavi igrati, Preporučeno za tebe, Otkrij nešto novo and Jackpoti are personalised. Item-item collaborative filtering and a day-to-day sequence model propose ~200 candidates; a trained scikit-learn logistic regression re-ranks them. On the tail — outside the global top 50, where 76.5% of discovery happens — it beats popularity 4.28× on NDCG@10 while reaching 732 games instead of 29.

Nove igre uses 12 months of history to find titles genuinely first released in the last three months — not merely games with no data. Badges come from the same source: NOVO, JACKPOT, U PORASTU (rising stake momentum).

Every tile's reason is the literal top contributor to its score. A dashed, dimmed tile is a game whose title is not in the data — shown only in your own history, never recommended. Full method in docs/evaluation.md.
